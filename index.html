🚀 Publish Your Game

Made a game? Put it online free so anyone can play — in about 2 minutes! 🌍

1
Download your game as an HTML file (from the Game Maker).
2
Click below to open Netlify Drop — free, no account needed to start!
3
Drag your file onto the page — it gives you a real link like yourgame.netlify.app
4
Share that link with friends — they click it and play! 🎉

Tip: putting stuff online is a great thing to do with a grown-up!
